My First Post

October 4, 2025 By JackSmack1971 announcement meta

Welcome to my new blog and portfolio site! I'm excited to finally have a dedicated space to share my thoughts, projects, and learnings.

Why I Created This Site

For a while now, I've wanted a central place to document my journey as a developer and creator. This site serves multiple purposes:

  • Documentation: A place to document what I learn and build
  • Sharing: Contributing back to the community that has helped me
  • Portfolio: Showcasing my work and projects
  • Connection: Meeting and collaborating with like-minded individuals

What to Expect

On this blog, you can expect to find:

  • Technical tutorials and guides
  • Project updates and behind-the-scenes looks
  • Thoughts on technology, development, and creativity
  • Resources and tools I find useful

Built with Modern Web Standards

This site itself is a project I'm proud of. It's built using:

  • Semantic HTML5 for proper structure
  • Modern CSS with Grid and Flexbox layouts
  • Vanilla JavaScript for interactivity
  • GitHub Pages for free, reliable hosting

The entire site is accessible, responsive, and follows web best practices. I'll be writing more about the technical decisions and architecture in future posts.

Join Me on This Journey

Whether you're a fellow developer, a student learning to code, or simply someone interested in technology, I hope you'll find something valuable here. Feel free to reach out through the contact page if you have questions, suggestions, or just want to connect!

Thanks for visiting, and stay tuned for more content!
